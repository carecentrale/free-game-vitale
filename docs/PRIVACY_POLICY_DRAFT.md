## Who this is for
Adventurers of Drenerics is a block-building game for children and families. Children do not create accounts and do not sign in. Only a parent or guardian can create an account or sign in, and only a parent can make purchases (if any are added later).

## Parents and sign-in
- A parent can sign in with their email address. We send a one-time code to that email; no password is stored.
- Connecting an account requires the parent to sign in with a Google account. This is used to check that the person connecting is an adult and the parent. We receive only what Google provides for that check (the parent's name and email address). We do not receive the child's details from Google.

- Children are never asked to sign in with Google or any other account.
- A parent sign-in is used for the parent area and the stats page. It is not used by the child to play.

## What the game stores
On the device:
- The saved game (the world, the character and the progress), stored only on the device the game is played on.

On our servers (only when a parent or a family uses a feature that needs it):
- Shared worlds: the world data, found by a code. Anyone with the code can open the world. There are no public lists of worlds.
- Cloud save: the save data, found by a code.
- Gameplay records: game events and errors, such as a level reached or a crash. These records do not include a child's name, email, location, photos or contacts, and they are not linked to a real identity.
- Parent account: the parent's email address and sign-in records.

## What the game does not collect
- No name, email, address, location, photos, contacts or other personal information from a child.
- No chat or free-typed names between players. Player names are generated by the game and can be changed from a preset list.
- No advertising identifiers and no tracking of children. [DECIDE: confirm if ads are added later, they will use only child-safe ad networks with no personalized ads.]

## Achievements
- Achievements are saved on the device, under a random local ID that the game creates. They do not include a name or any other personal information.
- When a parent connects an account, the achievements on that device are moved to a child profile under the parent's account. Connecting is only possible through the parent area, behind an adult-only check.
- Connecting always requires the parent's Google sign-in (see Parents and sign-in). Children never sign in with any account.

## How long we keep data
- Achievements and saved games: kept for as long as the game is installed and the save is kept on the device, and for the same period on the parent account once connected.
