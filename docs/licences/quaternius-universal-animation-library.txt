Checked: 24 Nov 2026

What it is: 7 animation clips (Idle_Loop, Walk_Loop, Jog_Fwd_Loop, Sword_Attack, Punch_Cross, Hit_Chest, Death01) taken from Quaternius "Universal Animation Library" (free Standard version, file UAL1_Standard.glb). No mesh, no texture: skeleton and clips only.
Why: www/models/imp.glb and www/models/puglin.glb have no clips. Their bones carry the same names as this library (Quaternius universal rig: neck_01, hand_l, index_01_l and so on; 55 of 55 imp bones match), so three.js plays these clips on them directly by bone name. Pinky and ball_leaf tracks were removed (the imp and puglin have no such bones).
