# Accounts Checklist

Claude cannot create accounts, enter passwords, or enter payment or identity details for you. You do these steps yourself, and Claude can walk you through each one. Keep all logins in a password manager and do not share them in chat.

| # | Account | Cost | Needed for | Status |
|---|---------|------|------------|--------|
| 1 | Google account (dedicated one for the game, e.g. yourname.kidsgames@gmail.com) | Free | Play Console, Drive, all Google services | [ ] |
| 2 | Google Play Console developer account | $25 USD one-time | Publishing the Android app | [ ] |
| 3 | GitHub account | Free | Storing the code, hosting the privacy policy | [ ] |
| 4 | Netlify or Cloudflare Pages account | Free tier | Hosting the playable web version | [ ] |
| 5 | Privacy policy page | Free (GitHub Pages) | Required by Play Store | [ ] |

Apple Developer account: deferred. Not needed for the Play Store release.

## Notes

- **Identity verification:** Google Play requires you to verify your identity with a government ID. Only you can do this step.
- **Your son's accounts:** Do not create accounts in his name. Accounts should be under a parent or guardian.
- **Payment:** The $25 fee is paid by you directly in Google Play Console.
- **Tester group:** New personal developer accounts must recruit at least 12 testers (you can use family and friends) for a 14-day closed test before public release. Check the current rule in Play Console when you sign up.
