Terms of Use

Adventurers of Drenerics. Last updated: 25 November 2026

These terms are an agreement between us and the parent or guardian of each child who plays Adventurers of Drenerics (the "Game"). A parent or guardian accepts them for their child. Children are not asked to agree to anything.

These terms apply when the Game is played on our website or on any other device.

Who can play

The Game is made for children and their families. Children should play with a parent or guardian nearby. Parents and guardians are responsible for how their children use the Game, and for any sign-in on the family's behalf.

The Game is not directed at children to sell them anything. It has no advertising and no in-app purchases.

What the Game is

The Game is a block-building adventure. Players can dig, build, collect gems, grow stronger, fight friendly creatures and share a world with friends by using a world code. The Game runs in a web browser today and is planned for Android and other app stores. When downloaded from an app store, these terms apply together with that store's terms.

Playing fairly and safely

  • Do not try to break, hack, copy or overload the Game or its servers.
  • Do not try to get into another person's saved game or cloud save.
  • Do not share a world code publicly. World codes are meant for friends and family.
  • Do not use the Game to harass anyone, or to share anything that is unkind, unsafe or meant for adults.

Playing together in shared worlds

Everything a player builds in a shared world can be seen by everyone in that world. Players must not build or spell anything rude, mean, scary or meant for adults, or copy other people's characters or logos, and must not use the Game to bother, trick or upset other players. Children can report what they see and hide a player for the rest of a visit. We look at every report; we may remove what was built and may keep a player out of shared worlds for a while. A parent can turn playing together off in the parent area.

Text written by AI

The fun facts about birds in the Game are written by an AI system (Claude, made by Anthropic) on our server, not on your child's device, and each one is checked by a second AI request before any child can see it. They are the same for every player: nothing about your child is sent to the AI, and your child cannot write to it or talk with it. AI can still make mistakes. Next to every AI-written line there is a small flag: a tap reports it to us and hides it on that device, and we look at every report.

Accounts and sign-in

Children do not need an account to play. A parent may sign in to save the game to the cloud, to turn playing together on or off, or to see the parent portal. A parent is responsible for keeping their sign-in details safe.

A parent can ask us to delete their account and data at any time, as explained in the Privacy Policy.

Your saved game

The game is saved in your browser on your device. If you clear your browser's data, or use a different device, the saved game may be lost unless you turned on cloud save. We are not responsible for lost progress.
