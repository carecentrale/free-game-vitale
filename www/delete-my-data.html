Delete my data

This page is for parents and guardians.

Here a parent who has signed in to the game can delete everything the game holds online, without opening the game. If you still have the game, you can do the same there: open Settings and tap Delete my data. That also removes the saved games on that device.

What is deleted

  • Your cloud save.
  • Your parent account and its sign-in.
  • Reports sent from a device are deleted when Delete my data is used in the game on that device.

What is not deleted

  • Saved games kept on a phone, tablet or computer. This page cannot reach them: use Delete my data in the game's Settings on that device, or clear the game's data in the browser or app settings.
  • Gameplay records and error reports. They are kept under one-visit codes and cannot be tied to a person. They are removed after 12 months.
  • Shared worlds. They are kept, so friends do not lose them. To have one removed, write to us from the Contact page with its code.
Step 1: sign in

Type the email address of your parent account. We send it a sign-in code.

Step 2: confirm, three times

You are signed in. Your cloud save and your parent account will be deleted.

This cannot be undone.

Once deleted, the cloud save and the parent account cannot be brought back. To go on, press and hold the button for 3 seconds.

Last check

Type the word DELETE in capital letters.

Step 3: the code

We sent a new 6-digit code to your sign-in address. Type it here within 15 minutes. Nothing has been deleted yet.

Your data has been deleted.

You are signed out. Thank you for playing.

Cancelling, closing this page or letting the 15 minutes pass deletes nothing. Five wrong codes cancel the request. Questions: the Contact page.
