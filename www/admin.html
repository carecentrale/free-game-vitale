Cloud save (this account)
Deletes the game saved to the cloud for the account you are signed in with. The game on each device is not changed. Automatic cloud saves stay off until a game presses Save now.
Bird facts (owner only)
Fun facts about the game's birds, written by AI on the server and checked by a second AI request. Only approved facts reach a game. Nothing about any player is sent to the AI. A fact flagged by 3 different visits is held until you look at it.

Mirelo (owner only)
The game reads these when it opens. They start at 15, 3, 24, 5 and 2. Only the owner's account can change them. They hold nothing about any player.
Castle spacing (owner only)
One castle about every X blocks, from 250 to 2000 (it starts at 500). A change affects only worlds started afterwards; worlds already started keep their castles where they are. Only the owner's account can change it. It holds nothing about any player.
Merchant prices (owner only)
Everything merchants sell and buy: the amount and its price in gems (amount 1 to 64, price 1 to 500). A merchant's list of goods does not change, only prices and amounts. Guards follow the prices in force. Gems only: no real money. Only the owner's account can change it. It holds nothing about any player.

Merchant traffic (owner only)
The most hover carts drawn on screen at once, from 1 to 20 (it starts at 6). If more carts are nearby, the closest ones are drawn. Saving changes it for every player the next time their game opens. Only the owner's account can change it. It holds nothing about any player.
Merchant guards (owner only)
The most guards a cart can have (0 to 6, it starts at 4; 0 turns guards off), and the gem worth of the goods a cart needs for each guard (it starts at 30, 40, 50, 58; each bigger than the one before, 1 to 500). Steps 5 and 6 are each 8 gems more than the step before. Only the owner's account can change it. It holds nothing about any player.

Forced update (owner only)
Force the update when a new version has waited this many days. A number from 0 to 180 (it starts at 30). 0 means every new version is forced at once. Before that, the Android app offers the new version on Google Play's own small window and downloads it while the child plays. Saving changes it for every player the next time their game opens. Only the owner's account can change it. It holds nothing about any player.
Quiz badges (owner only)
A child earns a badge at each of these numbers of right quiz answers, maths and words together (it starts at 1, 5, 25, 100 and 500). Each number must be bigger than the one before. Games pick up a change the next time they open; badges already reached stay earned. Only the owner's account can change it. It holds nothing about any player.

Share reward (owner only)
The gems a game gets, once, when a grown-up taps Share the game in the parent area or on this page (1 to 5000, it starts at 500). With the reward off, the buttons still share but say nothing about gems. Games pick up a change the next time they open. Only the owner's account can change it. It holds nothing about any player.
Teacher interest (owner only)
Teachers who asked, on the For teachers page, to hear when the teacher area opens. Only checked emails are on the list. Kept until the person unsubscribes or asks to be deleted; delete a row within 10 business days of a request. Never shared or sold.
