What is new

Adventurers of Drenerics. What has changed in each version, newest first. The newest version is 0.6.98.

New to the game, or looking for how something works? Read How to play.

0.6.98 Bird facts

  • Tap a bird to learn a fun fact about it, with new facts all the time!
  • If a fact looks wrong, tap the little flag and a grown-up will check it.

0.6.97 Birds everywhere

  • Birds everywhere! Parrots in the rainforest, penguins in the snow, ducks on the lakes, owls in the trees and more.
  • Walk up to them and they move away, then settle again a little further off. Birds are just for watching.

0.6.96 Monsters that move like real creatures

  • All the monsters have new looks and move like real creatures. Brutes stomp, Imps bounce and Goblins scurry!
  • They turn to face the way they walk, flinch when you hit them and fall over when they are beaten. Fights work as before.

0.6.95 Dragons rest and catch fish

  • Dragons now rest on castles, bridges and mountain tops, and dive into the water to catch fish!

0.6.94 Dragons that fly like real dragons

  • The dragons now fly like real dragons, and no two are the same! Some fly with their babies, and a few are very rare.
  • They fly head first, lean into their turns, beat their wings to climb and glide down with their wings spread.

0.6.93 Mirelo, the wandering mage

  • Meet Mirelo, a wandering mage! When you are ready, he teaches you to cast fireballs, and he stops by with magic tips.
  • While he visits he walks along with you for a little while. Tap Come back later and he stays away for a day.
  • Settings has a new switch: Tips from Mirelo.

0.6.92 Delete my data

  • New in Settings: Delete my data. A grown-up can remove everything the game holds. It asks three times and sends a code by email first.
  • The website has a Delete my data page too, for a grown-up who no longer has the game.

0.6.91 Report anything that looks wrong

  • See something that looks wrong? Tap the flag to tell us, or hide a player in a shared world.
  • In a shared world, open Settings and tap Report something wrong. Pick what it is about and what is wrong: taps only, no typing.
  • After reporting a player you can hide them: you will not see them until you leave the world.
  • Grown-ups can see the reports sent from this game in the parent area.

0.6.90 Playing together, safely

  • Grown-ups can now turn playing together on or off, and a short card reminds you how to play safely with friends.
  • The loading screen shows a short safety tip each time the game opens.
  • In the parent area, Open parent stats is now called Open parent portal, and only Sign in with Google is shown.
  • A saved game that was in a shared world opens in that world again. With no internet you play on your own until it is back, then the game joins by itself.

0.6.89 Game tips go in the Activity log

  • Short game messages, like "Too far away! Get closer.", no longer show across the top of the screen. They show for a moment beside the speech bubble at the bottom left and stay in the Activity log to read again.
  • The same message again adds to its line (for example "x3") instead of filling the log.

0.6.88 Share the game, the Activity log and a new website

  • Grown-ups can share the game from the parent area, and the game gets 500 gems as a thank-you.
  • The list of who joined and left is now called the Activity log.
  • The website has a new look: the same menu on every page, bigger text, a dark mode that follows your device, pictures of the game on the home page, an easier How to play guide, and a picture when you share a link.
  • A new page tells teachers and schools what is coming.

0.6.87 Playing together: no more starting in the ground, an activity log and a map

  • When you join a friend's world you now start on top of the land at the world's start point, not inside the ground
  • A speech bubble at the bottom left lights up when a friend joins or leaves your world. Tap it to read the list of everything that happened, and tap it again to close it
  • The top left shows how many players are in your world
  • Press M, or tap the Map button, to see a map of the land around you and where your friends are. Tap the map or press M again to close it

0.6.86 Real bridges

  • Bridges now look like real ones: little wooden footbridges over streams, stone arch bridges, and long timber bridges with stone towers over wide water. They are wider too, so they are easy to walk across
  • Cream and sandstone castles no longer flicker when you stand right next to their walls

0.6.85 Guards tidy up

  • Monsters the merchants' guards drive off no longer lie around on the road: they sink into the ground after a few seconds

0.6.84 Log off

  • The quit question has a new Log off button. It saves your game and takes you back to the start menu, so you can load another game or world, or start a new one

0.6.83 A castle to welcome you, and a quicker start

  • A castle now greets you each time the game opens, while it gets ready and checks for a new version
  • The start menu has two big buttons: Load my game (the one you played last) and Start a new game. All your saved games are still there, folded below
  • Tick "Load my game by itself next time" to go straight into your game. Change it any time in Settings

0.6.82 Castles without flicker

  • Castle walls and windows no longer flicker or blink when you look at them from far away. Up close, blocks look as sharp as before

0.6.81 Grand castles, roads, guards, dragons and playing together

  • Castles are much grander: three styles, tall roofs, towers with spires, windows, gardens and furnished rooms. Your own worlds get them too
  • Roads now wind across the land and join every castle. Follow the signposts, cross the bridges, and dig up cobblestones for your own paths!
  • Rich merchants now travel with guards on foot, on horses and on hover sleds, and they fight off monsters. More guards means better goods!
  • Friendly dragons now fly over the land. Watch them swoop down to munch a tree!
  • Playing together is better: in a shared world everyone now sees the same merchants, monsters and treasure, and you can fight side by side
  • Grown-ups: the parent area has a new Dragons part: On, Stay high or Off

0.6.80 The game offers each new version

  • When there is a new version, the game offers it. Tap Update, keep playing, then tap Restart. Your world stays safe

0.6.79 Quiz badges are easy to find

  • Tap Powers to see your quiz badges: five big stars that light up as you earn them, and how many right answers until the next one

0.6.78 Play again, quiz badges and merchants on the move

  • After a right answer the quiz stays open: tap Play again for another question from the same friend, or Done when you have had enough
  • Quiz badges: earn one for your 1st, 5th, 25th, 100th and 500th right answer. See them in Powers
  • Merchants no longer stay parked at a castle: if you do not trade, a merchant waits a little, then rides on to the next castle

0.6.77 Busy castles and travelling merchants

  • Castles seen from far away no longer look cut off or floating: they stand on their hill all the way until you arrive
  • Castle courtyards are busy now: market stalls with their keepers, a well, barrels, crates, hay carts, banners and lanterns, and villagers walking about
  • Merchants ride hover carts from castle to castle. Walk up to one and it stops: tap the cart to trade. Sell monster parts and blocks for gems, and buy gear and castle blocks with gems

0.6.76 First-person view, caves and castles

  • New View button: see the world through your character's eyes, and look up and down. Indoors, the camera now stays close so you can always see
  • Caves! Find a cave mouth in the hills and walk in to explore tunnels, big rooms and pools. New worlds have them
  • Castles! Find one, walk through the gate, climb the towers and look for the treasure room. New worlds have them

0.6.75 Hint and Another question

  • Quiz friends have two new buttons: Hint gives you a clue, and Another question swaps it for a different one
  • For parents: the quiz friends report also counts how many hints and swaps were used

0.6.74 Aim the fireball, dig by hand, and a Powers button

  • The fireball now flies where you tap: tap the Spell button, then tap a monster or a place. The Spell button is no longer hidden under the Recent list
  • Swords now reach 2 blocks, so walk up to a monster to hit it. Fireballs still fly far
  • You can dig without a pickaxe now. It is slower, so keep your pickaxe for big jobs
  • Your powers now live behind the Powers button, so the top left of the screen is clear

0.6.73 Meet Vital the elephant

  • Meet Vital the elephant! He walks up with a ! and shows you how to play, one tip at a time. Tap No more tips if you know it all
  • Settings has a "Tips from Vital" switch to turn his tips off or on

0.6.72 100 Maths levels and 100 Words levels

  • Quiz friends now have 100 Maths levels and 100 Words levels. Answer right and your level goes up, with bigger sums and longer words to spell
  • Each question shows your Maths level or your Words level. A wrong answer never takes a level away
  • Short words come with a picture. From 6 letters on you spell the word with no picture
  • Grown-ups: the parent area has a new Learning level part, to keep the questions in one range of levels, set a level, or start the levels again

0.6.71 Tap the question mark

  • Tapping the "?" bubble over a quiz friend now opens its question, the same as tapping the friend
  • A tap outside the bag, Settings or the quit question closes it
  • The Recent list has a shorter heading

0.6.69 Tidier buttons at the top

  • The Settings and Controls buttons no longer sit on top of each other

0.6.68 Hold a quick bar slot to fill it

  • Press and hold a quick bar slot for a moment: the bag opens, and the item you tap goes into that slot
  • On tablets and phones, holding a hand button does the same for slots 1 and 2

0.6.67 Quiz friends come sooner

  • A quiz friend comes to say hello as soon as you start playing
  • After you answer a question right, the next friend comes after about a minute of moving around
  • If a friend leaves without a right answer, the next one comes 4 to 7 minutes later, as before

0.6.66 Tap to hit, and life bars only on hurt monsters

  • With the sword in your hand, tap a close monster to hit it once. Before, you had to hold. Holding still keeps hitting
  • A monster shows its life bar only after it has been hit

0.6.65 Word scrambles, and a quiz report for parents

  • Quiz friends now also ask you to spell a word: a picture shows what it is, and its letters are mixed up
  • Tap the letters in the right order. Tap a letter you placed to take it back
  • A right word gives 5 gems, or 2 gems after a wrong try. After two wrong tries the first letter is put in place for you
  • About half the questions are words and half are maths
  • For parents: the parent area shows how many questions were seen and answered right, for maths and for words

0.6.64 Life bars for monsters

  • Every monster has a small life bar over its head. It shows how much health the monster has left
  • The bar is green at first, turns yellow as the monster gets weaker, and red when it is nearly defeated

0.6.63 Quiz friends

  • Now and then a friendly owl, turtle or robot walks up to you with a "?" over its head and waves
  • Tap it to get one easy maths question with three answers. A right answer gives 5 gems, or 2 gems after a wrong try
  • A wrong answer takes nothing away: try again, and after two tries a picture helps you
  • There is no timer. Tap "Not now" if you want to keep playing
  • Settings has a "Quiz friends" switch to turn them off or on

0.6.62 Building on phones, and room in a full bag

  • Bag full? Open the bag and tap Drop next to a block or a monster part. One goes on the ground nearby, where you can pick it up again for 5 minutes. Gems, tools and weapons cannot be dropped
  • With a block in your hand, tap the side or top of a block to build there. Before, a tap only tried to dig, so touch screens could not build

0.6.61 Sounds and music

  • Sounds for digging, building, gems, jumping, landing, sword swings and hits, fireballs, teleporting, water, broken gear and buttons
  • Calm classical music plays while you play: "Morning Mood" by Edvard Grieg
  • Change how loud they are in Settings, with the Sounds and Music sliders

0.6.60 Hand buttons, and getting ready for sound

  • Quit has moved into Settings: the "Quit the game" button is at the bottom
  • Tablets and phones: two hand buttons above JUMP show quick bar slots 1 and 2. Tap one to hold that item
  • A Spell button casts the fireball. It replaces the FIRE button
  • Tap a close monster to swing a sword that is on a hand button, even with the pickaxe in your hand
  • Settings has Sounds and Music sliders, and a list of music and sound credits
  • The sounds and the music themselves arrive in a later version

0.6.59 Quit the game

  • New Quit button at the top, under Settings. It asks first, then saves your game and closes it
  • On a computer, Esc opens the same question (Esc still closes the bag or Settings first)
  • On Android, the phone's Back button opens the question too, and Save and quit closes the app

0.6.58 Moving monsters, new scenery

  • The Imp and the Spike Beast now move their legs, arms and head when they walk and look around when they stand
  • Fixed: only one Imp and one Spike Beast could be seen at a time. Now every one of them shows
  • The other monsters swing their arms and legs when they walk
  • New scenery made in the game: cactuses in the desert, ice crystals and snowmen in the tundra, pumpkins in the grassland and forest

0.6.57 Phone controls

  • New: a thumb stick to walk. Push it a little to walk slowly, all the way to run
  • On phones, held either way, the buttons are smaller and fit the screen
  • On phones, the quick bar and power meters are hidden. Tap the button at the top left to show them; choosing a tool hides them again

0.6.56 Safer shared worlds

  • Shared worlds are better protected: a world can only be opened or saved with its code
  • The game no longer keeps a code on the device to count play. Each visit is counted on its own

0.6.55 Standing still

  • Fixed: the character's head bobbed up and down while standing still. The landing squash now happens only when the character really lands

0.6.54 Steady picture

  • Fixed: the ground and the character shook slightly while moving. The world is now drawn steadily, and the camera follows smoothly even when the device is busy

0.6.53 Biomes

  • The world is split into biomes (desert, savanna, rainforest, grassland, forest, taiga, tundra) that blend into each other across the world
  • Deserts are sand, tundra is snow (new snow block), and each biome has its own trees and plants

0.6.52 Lighter scenery, performance readout

  • Scenery is stored more compactly and is drawn only near the player
  • Add ?perf=1 to the page address to see frame rate and memory in the console (stays on your device)

0.6.51 Water puts out fireballs

  • A fireball that reaches water fizzles out in a puff of steam, with no blast and no damage
  • Enemies still take the blast when a fireball hits them

0.6.50 Trees, bushes and scenery on the grass

  • Trees, bushes, grass tufts, flowers, mushrooms, stones and stumps from the free CC0 Low Poly Nature Pack by flo-bit now grow on the grass
  • Scenery does not block walking or digging; it is decoration only
  • Fireballs aimed at water now burst on the water instead of passing through it

0.6.49 Fireball hits the block you click

  • A right-click fireball now flies straight at the block or enemy you click, so it lands where you aimed

0.6.48 Fireball aims at what you click

  • A fireball flies to the point you click or tap, and it follows an enemy you hit. It lasts long enough to reach it
  • Fire sparks burst where a fireball lands. Any part of an enemy's body counts as a hit

0.6.47 Fireball controls

  • Right-click on a computer casts a fireball when the fireball is the selected quick bar item. Otherwise right-click still places a block
  • Tablets and phones get a FIRE button to the right of the quick bar (shown with on-screen controls on, once you have learned the fireball)

0.6.46 Climbing out of water

  • Fixed: from the water you could not get out onto a shore. Now, holding jump against the shore lifts you over the edge

0.6.45 Durability, gear and enemy parts

  • Pickaxes and weapons have durability. A pickaxe wears by one each block it digs; a weapon wears by one each hit. At zero it breaks
  • Enemies drop parts (ears, horns, hides...) and sometimes gear: weapons and armour, with better gear from stronger enemies
  • Armour is in the bag with durability. It does nothing yet

0.6.44 Enemy names, health and weaknesses

  • Each enemy has its own name, power level, health and toughness. Some are weak to fire (goblins, brutes, hood goblins) or to holy light (imps, spear imps)
  • The sword hits for your Fighting level; each hit takes health off, and an enemy is defeated at zero health
  • Fireball hurts every enemy inside its burst. Enemies weak to fire take double damage

0.6.43 Swimming and the first magic skill

  • Water: you swim. In water you move slower and sink slowly. Hold jump to swim up
  • At Fighting level 2 you unlock magic and choose a skill. The first skill is Fireball
  • Fireball: tap the screen (or press F) to shoot. It defeats the monsters it hits. Level 2 magic makes a bigger ball and a wider burst

0.6.42 Lakes, sand and a 4000 x 4000 land

  • Water fills low places: lakes and the sea, with sand on the shore. You can wade in water, and dig or build under it
  • The playable land is 4000 x 4000 blocks around the start point
  • Land is drawn in chunks around the player and loads a few chunks at a time, so the game does not freeze while you walk

0.6.41 Saved games list fixed

  • Saved games made since version 0.6.34 are listed again at start (they were hidden by mistake)

0.6.40 Taller mountains and deeper valleys

  • The world is 96 blocks tall: higher mountains and deeper valleys and canyons
  • Worlds saved in the 0.6.39 height keep their changes

0.6.39 Sky, sun, clouds and mountains

  • A sky with a gradient, a sun and drifting clouds
  • Hills and mountain ranges with sharp ridges; the world is taller
  • Saved worlds keep their changes

0.6.38 Enemies start near a loaded game

  • When a saved game is opened, the first enemies now appear where the player is, not at a random spot

0.6.37 Fix: game would not start after a saved game was chosen

  • Fixed a bug from version 0.6.34 that stopped the game from showing the world: some game settings and the bag were missing

0.6.36 Errors shown on screen

  • If something goes wrong, the error message appears at the bottom of the screen instead of failing silently

0.6.35 World status for support

  • The parent area shows the world status (seed, blocks drawn, player position, ground height), and drawing errors are logged

0.6.34 A 400,000 x 400,000 block world

  • The world is now 400,000 blocks wide. Land is made on demand from the seed, so walking anywhere works
  • Only the blocks you change are saved and shared. Old 40 x 40 saves and shared worlds move into the middle of the new world
  • Only the blocks near you are drawn, and blocks buried in the ground are skipped

0.6.33 A new random island for every new game

  • Every new game gets its own island: hills, caves and gems are different each time
  • The land slopes down toward the rim, so the island edge looks like land instead of a straight wall

0.6.32 Saved games list layout fix

  • Fixed the saved games list at start, which was squeezed into a narrow column

0.6.31 Clearer saved games list

  • The start menu shows each saved game as a card: name, world code and last played time, with Play and Delete under it

0.6.30 Safety limit of 100 saved games on the device

  • Up to 100 saved games on this device. A safety limit; the start menu says when it is reached

0.6.29 One free cloud save; unlimited saves on the device

  • Saved games on this device are unlimited
  • The cloud save holds one game per parent account. Automatic saves never replace a different game
  • Pressing Save now asks before it replaces the game in the cloud

0.6.28 Parents can delete the cloud save

  • In the parent area, a signed-in parent can delete their cloud save. The game on this device is not changed
  • After a delete, automatic cloud saves stay off until the parent presses Save now

0.6.27 Choose a saved game at start

  • When you open the game, your saved games are listed. Pick one to carry on, delete one, or start a new game
  • Up to 10 saved games on this device. Your old save is kept as the first one
  • Starting a new game from the bag keeps your other saved games

0.6.26 Dropped items disappear after 5 minutes

  • Items dropped on the ground (when the bag is full) disappear 5 minutes after they were dropped

0.6.25 Stuck defeated creatures disappear after 5 minutes

  • A defeated creature that is hovering or still falling now sinks and disappears 5 minutes after it was defeated, the same as one lying on the ground

0.6.24 Enemy log records dug-out bodies

  • When the ground under a defeated creature is dug out, the event is recorded in the enemy log (no names, no player codes)

0.6.23 Defeated creatures no longer hover

  • A defeated creature that lies on a block is placed exactly on top of it, so it no longer hangs in the air

0.6.22 Fallen creatures fall when ground is dug away

  • A defeated creature that is left with nothing under it (for example after the ground is dug out) now falls to the ground

0.6.21 Fallen creatures drop off the map

  • A defeated creature that lands past the edge of the island now falls off the map instead of hanging in the air

0.6.20 Home page

  • The game now lives at its own address (/play/), with a home page at the main address

0.6.19 Teleport works in the background

  • The teleport cast finishes on time even when you switch to another tab

0.6.18 Teleport lands on top of the land

  • Teleporting now puts you on top of the land, not at the bottom of a hole you dug
  • Enemies that leave the map are recorded for the grown-up admin log

0.6.17 Fixes for enemies off the map

  • Enemies that fall off the island or get stuck in the air are removed; new ones appear on the grass
  • A defeated enemy that was in the air falls and lands on the block below it
  • The teleport button counts down while it is casting

0.6.16 Easier to close Settings

  • Press Esc to close Settings
  • Tap outside the Settings panel to close it
  • The Close button is larger

0.6.15 Teleport to the start

  • Purple teleport button at the top right takes you back to the start, safely above the ground
  • Teleporting is free. Cast it for 5 minutes, then wait 1 hour before using it again

0.6.14 Enemy fixes and corpses

  • Enemies stay on the ground while they walk and no longer lift off the blocks
  • Enemies turn back at the edge of a ledge instead of walking off it
  • Defeated enemies lie on the ground for 5 minutes, then sink into the ground
  • Right-click to build with a block from the quick bar, without selecting it first

0.6.13 Settings screen

  • Settings button at the top right shows the game version and what has changed

0.6.12 Drops when the bag is full

  • When the bag is full, digging still works. The block drops on the ground as a small spinning cube
  • Walk next to a dropped item to pick it up once there is room in the bag
  • You can't build where something is on the ground until you pick it up
  • Up to 40 items can lie on the ground at once
  • Dropped items stay on this device only

0.6.11 Cloud save for grown-ups

  • Grown-ups can save the game to the cloud and load it on another device
  • Sign-in is for grown-ups only

0.6.10 Parent area and player review

  • A grown-up area with a stats page for seeing play activity

0.6.9 Enemy movement

  • Enemies bounce and sway while walking and breathe gently while standing

0.6.8 Bestiary monsters

  • Imp and Spike Beast enemies now have real 3D models
  • Models load when first needed; a placeholder block shows until they arrive
  • Other enemies keep their original shapes

0.6.0 Save and shared worlds

  • Your character and world save on this device automatically
  • Reopening the game restores your name, bag, quick bar, power levels, position, and the world
  • The bag has "Start a new game" (asks first, then erases saved progress on this device)

0.5.1 Phone layout

  • On phones, the quick bar sits above the controls and fits the screen width

0.5.0 Quick bar and bag

  • Quick bar of 6 slots at the bottom; select with 1-6 or tap
  • Recent panel on the right: tap an item to put it in the selected slot
  • Bag (B key or button): all items, tap one to put it in the selected slot
  • Pickaxe digs, sword fights, and blocks build, depending on the selected slot
  • Slime defeats and digging add items to the bag

  • Blocks have textures: grass, dirt, stone with cracks, and sparkling gems
  • Character has a face, hair, shoes, belt, and shading
  • Mouse wheel and two-finger pinch to zoom
  • Head turns toward the mouse or finger; drag sideways to turn the world
  • Slimes to fight: hold on one to attack it, then get Fighting XP and a gem

0.3.0 Limbs and progressive digging

  • Character has head, body, arms, legs, and a pickaxe
  • Digging is held, with a crack that grows as progress builds
