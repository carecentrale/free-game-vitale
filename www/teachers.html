For teachers and schools

A block-building adventure your class can learn in. Teachers will add their own questions, and each class will play in its own private world. The teacher area is not open yet.

What teachers will be able to do

Your own quiz questions

Write quiz questions for your class. The quiz friends ask them while your students play.

A private world for each class

Each class gets its own world with a longer 8-character code. Nobody can find it in a list or join without the code.

A fresh start every school year

Class worlds are cleared at the end of each school year, so nothing is kept longer than it needs to be.

Learning that follows each child

Quiz friends ask maths and word questions at each child's own level, from 1 to 100.

Who can join

Teachers at schools in Canada and the United States. We check that you teach at a school before your teacher account opens, and again every year.

What we never ask for

No student names and no class lists. Children never sign in. Parents add their own child to a class.
