Privacy Policy

Adventurers of Drenerics. Developer: Adventurers of Drenerics. Last updated: 25 November 2026 (Delete my data in the game and on this website; reports, stopping multiplayer and the Playing together choice added; sign-in with Google only. Before: 23 November 2026, gameplay records described by kind; error reports, your character in a shared world, Google Play, sharing the game, teachers who ask to hear from us, and Gmail added; bird facts written by AI, with Anthropic as a service provider for game content)

Adventurers of Drenerics is a block-building adventure game made by a small family project, for children and their families. It is available in a web browser now and is planned for Android and other app stores. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and how to ask us to delete it.

Short version
  • Children do not create accounts, do not sign in, and are not asked for their name, email, age, location, photos or contacts.
  • The game has no ads, no advertising networks, no third-party analytics and no in-app purchases.
  • Players cannot send messages to each other. Names are chosen from a preset list.
  • Parents can sign in to see their family's play statistics and to save the game to the cloud. Only parents sign in.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time. See How to delete your data.

Why we collect it

We collect only what the game needs to run, to save progress, to understand and fix problems, and to let a parent see their family's play. We do not use information about children for advertising, for profiles, or for any purpose outside the game.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads, and we do not use advertising networks or ad identifiers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, tracking or advertising software.
  • We do not sell information, and we do not share children's information with anyone for marketing.
  • We do not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions. If that changes, this policy and the store listing will be updated first, and a parent gate will be added before any purchase.
  • We do not let players send messages to each other.
  • We do not ask children to share photos, contacts, location or microphone or camera access.

Service providers

  • Vercel: hosts the game and the website.
  • Supabase: stores the records described above.
  • Google Play: the Android app asks Google Play whether a newer version of the game is available, when the game opens and when it comes back on screen. Nothing about the player is sent by the game with that question. Governed by Google's own privacy policy.
  • Gmail (Google): sends the 6-digit code and, later, news emails to teachers who ask to hear from us on the For teachers page, from our address yammay@gmail.com. Governed by Google's own privacy policy. Never used for children.
  • Anthropic: its AI system (Claude) writes and checks the fun facts about birds, when our server asks it to. We send it only the kind of bird, the part of the world, our writing rules and facts already written. Nothing about any child, player, device or game is sent, and nothing a child does in the game calls it.
  • jsDelivr: delivers the Three.js and Supabase code libraries the game loads. It receives the normal request details that any website receives.
  • Google and Facebook: only if a parent chooses to sign in with them. They are governed by their own privacy policies.

These providers may store information in countries other than the one where you live. We only use them to run the game.

How long we keep information

  • Enemy debug log: 30 days, then deleted automatically. A parent can change this period.
  • Gameplay records and error reports: 12 months, then deleted.
  • Reports: only as long as needed to deal with them and to spot repeats, up to 12 months (our team may set a shorter period), then deleted automatically. A stop on multiplayer ends at its end date, or when our team lifts it.
  • Shared worlds: until the family asks us to delete them, or until they have not been used for 12 months.
  • Parent sign-in and cloud save: until the parent deletes them, or asks us to delete the account.
  • Device data: stays on the device until the game's data is cleared in the browser or app settings.

Security

Information is sent between the game and our providers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Stored gameplay records can be read only by the family's parent account and by our team. Shared worlds can be opened by anyone with the world code. The game can open or save a shared world only by giving its code. Worlds cannot be listed.

Parents' rights

  • See what has been recorded for your family's devices, on the stats page.
  • Ask us to delete any record, or the whole family's data, as described below.
  • Stop further collection at any time: sign out, turn off cloud save, or stop using the game.
  • Ask a question or make a complaint, using the Contact page.

How to delete your data

In the game: a parent opens Settings and taps Delete my data. The game shows what will be removed and asks three times. If a parent is signed in, it then emails a 6-digit code to the sign-in address; the code works for 15 minutes. After that, the saved games, quiz friends reports and settings on that device, the reports sent from it, the cloud save and the parent account are deleted. This cannot be undone.

Without the game: use the Delete my data page. A parent signs in there and deletes the cloud save and the parent account, with the same three confirmations and emailed code.

  • The cloud save and the parent account are removed from our live database at once, and from backups within 30 days.
  • While we wait for the code, we keep a note that the account asked for deletion, for at most 15 minutes. The code itself is handled by the sign-in service.
  • After a deletion we keep its date only, with nothing that says who it was.
  • Not deleted this way: gameplay records and error reports, which are kept under one-visit codes and cannot be tied to a person, and shared worlds, which are kept so that friends do not lose them.

Anything else, for example a shared world: email yammay@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the world code. We confirm within 7 days and delete within 30 days.

Teachers who ask to hear from us

On the For teachers and schools page, a teacher or school staff member (18 or older) can ask to be emailed when the teacher area opens. This is for adults only, and no information about any child is asked for.

  • What we collect: name, school name, country (Canada or the United States) and school email, the wording of the consent box that was ticked, the date it was ticked and the date the email was checked.
  • Checking the email: we email a 6-digit code first. Nothing goes on the list until the right code is entered. A request whose code is never entered is deleted after 24 hours. The code is stored only in a scrambled form (a hash). To stop misuse, we keep for 24 hours when a code was sent, with the sender's network address in scrambled form only.
  • Why: only to tell you when the teacher area opens and to send news for teachers about Drenerics. The list is never shared or sold, and being on it does not create a teacher account: teacher accounts will have their own checks.
  • Who sends the emails: Adventurers of Drenerics, from yammay@gmail.com, through Gmail (Google). Every news email has a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • How long we keep it: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Unsubscribing deletes your details at once. Addresses that no longer receive email are removed once a year.
  • To be removed: use the unsubscribe link in any of our emails, or write to us through the Contact page; we delete it within 10 business days.

Children's privacy

The game is made for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not ask children for their name, email, age, location, photos or contacts. We are designed to meet the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families policy. This policy will be reviewed against both before public release. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts to collect different information, changes how the game is paid for, or starts to show ads, we will update this page first and change the date at the top. We will also update the information on the store listing.

Contact

Questions about privacy or deletion: see the Contact page.
