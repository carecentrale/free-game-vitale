Privacy Policy

Adventurers of Drenerics. Developer: [developer name exactly as shown on Google Play]. Last updated: 2 November 2026

Adventurers of Drenerics is a block-building adventure game made by a small family project, for children and their families. It is available in a web browser now and is planned for Android and other app stores. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and how to ask us to delete it.

Short version
  • Children do not create accounts, do not sign in, and are not asked for their name, email, age, location, photos or contacts.
  • The game has no ads, no advertising networks, no third-party analytics and no in-app purchases.
  • Players cannot send messages to each other. Names are chosen from a preset list.
  • Parents can sign in to see their family's play statistics and to save the game to the cloud. Only parents sign in.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time. See How to delete your data.

Why we collect it

We collect only what the game needs to run, to save progress, to understand and fix problems, and to let a parent see their family's play. We do not use information about children for advertising, for profiles, or for any purpose outside the game.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads, and we do not use advertising networks or ad identifiers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, tracking or advertising software.
  • We do not sell information, and we do not share children's information with anyone for marketing.
  • We do not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions. If that changes, this policy and the store listing will be updated first, and a parent gate will be added before any purchase.
  • We do not let players send messages to each other.
  • We do not ask children to share photos, contacts, location or microphone or camera access.

Service providers

  • Vercel: hosts the game and the website.
  • Supabase: stores the records described above.
  • jsDelivr: delivers the Three.js and Supabase code libraries the game loads. It receives the normal request details that any website receives.
  • Google and Facebook: only if a parent chooses to sign in with them. They are governed by their own privacy policies.

These providers may store information in countries other than the one where you live. We only use them to run the game.

How long we keep information

  • Enemy debug log: 30 days, then deleted automatically. A parent can change this period.
  • Gameplay records and error reports: 12 months, then deleted.
  • Shared worlds: until the family asks us to delete them, or until they have not been used for 12 months.
  • Parent sign-in and cloud save: until the parent deletes them, or asks us to delete the account.
  • Device data: stays on the device until the game's data is cleared in the browser or app settings.

Security

Information is sent between the game and our providers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Stored gameplay records can be read only by the family's parent account and by our team. Shared worlds can be opened by anyone with the world code. We are still tightening database access before public release.

Parents' rights

  • See what has been recorded for your family's devices, on the stats page.
  • Ask us to delete any record, or the whole family's data, as described below.
  • Stop further collection at any time: sign out, turn off cloud save, or stop using the game.
  • Ask a question or make a complaint, using the Contact page.

How to delete your data

To request deletion: email yammay@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Say which data you want deleted. Include the device code or world code if you have them, and, for a parent account, the email address you sign in with.

  • We will confirm receipt within 7 days.
  • We will delete the data within 30 days, and tell you when it is done.
  • Deleting a device's records also deletes any parent links to that device.
  • Deleting a parent account removes its cloud saves and its links. The stored device records stay with the device unless you also ask us to delete them.

To delete the game's saved data on a device without contacting us, clear the game's data in the browser or app settings on that device. In-app deletion will be added in a later update, and this page will be updated when it is.

Children's privacy

The game is made for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not ask children for their name, email, age, location, photos or contacts. We are designed to meet the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families policy. This policy will be reviewed against both before public release. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts to collect different information, changes how the game is paid for, or starts to show ads, we will update this page first and change the date at the top. We will also update the information on the store listing.
