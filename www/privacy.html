Privacy Policy

Adventurers of Drenerics. Last updated: 2 November 2026

Adventurers of Drenerics is a block-building adventure game made by a small family project, for children and their families. We designed it to collect as little information as possible. This page explains what the game stores, why, and who can see it.

Short version

  • Children do not create accounts and do not sign in.
  • We do not ask for a child's name, email address, age, location, photos or contacts.
  • Players cannot type names or messages to each other. Names are chosen from a preset list.
  • The game has no advertising and no purchases.
  • A parent can choose to sign in to save the game to the cloud. Only the parent signs in.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads, and we do not use advertising networks.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share information about children.
  • We do not sell anything in the game. There are no purchases.
  • We do not let players contact each other.

Services we use

  • Hosting: Vercel, which serves the game files.
  • Database: Supabase, which stores the records described above.
  • Libraries: Three.js and the Supabase library, loaded from the jsDelivr content network. Those services receive the normal request details that any website receives.
  • Sign-in (parents only): the email code, Google or Facebook sign-in that the parent chooses.

How long we keep information

  • Enemy debug log: 30 days, then automatically deleted. A parent can change this period.
  • Gameplay records and error reports: 12 months, then deleted.
  • Shared worlds: until the family asks us to delete them, or until they have not been used for 12 months.
  • Cloud saves: until the parent deletes them or asks us to delete the account.

A parent's choices

  • A parent can sign in to the stats page and see what has been recorded.
  • A parent can ask us to delete any record, including a device's records, by emailing the contact address on the Contact page.
  • A parent can sign out, or stop cloud save, at any time.
  • To remove a device's saved game, clear the game's data in the browser settings on that device.

Children's privacy

The game is made for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not ask children for their name, email, age, location or photos. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts to collect different information, or changes how ads or purchases work, we will update this page first and change the date at the top.
