Play

Free to play in your browser

Dig, build and explore a world of blocks

A block-building adventure for kids, families and anyone who loves to build. Play alone or in the same world as your friends.

Play now How to play

Play free in your browser on tablets, phones and computers, or get the Android app. No sign-in needed.

What you can do

Dig and build

Dig into the ground and build your own places with what you find.

Find gems and grow stronger

Collect gems, gain levels and learn magic.

Meet Vital and the quiz friends

Vital the elephant shows you how to play. Quiz friends ask maths and word questions for gems.

Play with friends

Share a 4-letter code and play together in your own private world.

Learn while playing

Transform screen time into learning

Quiz friends walk up during play with a maths or word question. The questions follow your child's level, so they stay just hard enough, and a right answer earns gems for the adventure.

  • 100 maths levels and 100 word levels
  • Levels follow your child and never go down
  • No timers on questions, no pressure
  • See your child's levels in the parent area

For parents and teachers

Fun at any age, with parents and teachers in control

See what your child is learning, choose the level, decide what is switched on, and choose who plays in your world.

Learning report

See how many maths and word questions your child answered, how many were right the first time, and how many hints they used.

Choose the level

Leave the level on Auto, keep it within a band between 1 and 100, or set it yourself. You can start again at any time.

Guides inside the game

Vital the elephant teaches new players how to play, one tip at a time.

Parent controls

Switch quiz friends and Vital's tips on or off in Settings. The dragons, the learning level and the cloud save are in the parent area, which children do not open by accident, and saving to the cloud needs a grown-up's sign-in.

🔒 ABCD

Private worlds, never public

Every world is private. Nobody can find it in a list or join it without its 4-letter code, so children only play with the people you invite. A teacher can start a world for the class and share the code with the students.

Coming soon

For teachers and schools

Approved teachers will add their own quiz questions for their class, and the quiz friends will ask them in the game. Each class gets its own private world with a longer 8-character code.
