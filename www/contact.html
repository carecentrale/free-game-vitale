Contact

Adventurers of Drenerics is made by a small family project. We are happy to hear from parents, guardians and players.

We aim to reply within 7 days.

Parents' suggestion box

Have an idea for the game? Write it below. When you press the button, your own email app opens with your suggestion ready to send. Nothing is stored on this website.

Please do not include a child's name or other personal details.

What to contact us about

  • Questions about privacy, or a request to see or delete a record (for example a device's play records or a shared world).
  • Reporting a problem in the Game, or content that should not be in it.
  • Questions about the terms of use.

Please do not send us

Please do not send a child's name, address, school, photo or other personal details in an email. We do not need them to help. If you have a question about a child's record, a parent can send it, and we will ask only for the device code and the world code, if needed.

Safety

If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number first.
